Marketplace research & software

Better market decisions begin with better data.

I build and operate private software that evaluates publicly available marketplace listings, standardizes product details, and identifies price and availability differences for further review.

Current operating model

Private research environment

The system supports controlled internal analysis. It is not a public marketplace or checkout service.

  • No public user accounts
  • No payment processing
  • No marketplace login collection

Research approach

Structured analysis, with clear data boundaries.

The software is designed to use authorized interfaces responsibly, request only information needed for the research task, and keep marketplace data separate from personal or payment information.

01

Retrieve

Request current product and listing details through authorized marketplace APIs and public sources.

02

Standardize

Normalize identifiers, condition, currency, shipping, and availability so comparable items can be evaluated consistently.

03

Review

Apply defined screening criteria and retain candidates for controlled observation before any business decision is made.

eBay integration

Focused on current listing information.

The proposed integration uses the eBay Browse API to retrieve fields such as item identifiers, titles, price, shipping, condition, and availability for internal marketplace comparison and research.

Independent operation

This website and research practice are independently operated and are not sponsored or endorsed by eBay Inc.

  • The website does not ask visitors to connect an eBay account or provide eBay credentials.
  • The research environment does not process buyer payments or perform an on-site eBay checkout.
  • The integration is limited to listing fields needed for comparison, availability checks, and quality control.
  • Internal strategies, supplier information, evaluation rules, and operational dashboards are not publicly exposed.
